Account Setup Guide

Follow along during orientation — bookmark this page for reference throughout the summer.

Before we begin: Have your preferred email address accessible and your personal devices ready. We'll walk through each section together, but please raise your hand or use the chat at any point if you fall behind.
1

WKU Account Management

Use the WKU Account Management portal to retrieve your credentials. You will need your last name, date of birth, and last four digits of your SSN.

The portal will display your:

  • WKU ID — your university identification number
  • NetID — used to log in to some university systems
  • TopperMail address — your official WKU email (format: firstname.lastname###@topper.wku.edu)

A password reset option is available on this page if needed. Have your information ready before clicking the link below.

Open WKU Account Management →

2

Setting Up Microsoft Authenticator

Multi-factor authentication is required to access your WKU accounts. You will need to install the Microsoft Authenticator app on your mobile device before logging in for the first time.

WKU Authenticator Setup Instructions (all devices) →

3

Accessing WKU TopperMail

Your WKU account includes access to Microsoft 365 applications, including Outlook, which you will use to access your TopperMail account.

  1. Go to office.com and click Sign In.

  2. Choose Work or school account.

  3. Enter your WKU TopperMail address and password.

  4. Authenticate using the Microsoft Authenticator app.

  5. You will land on the Copilot home page. Click Apps → More Apps and select Outlook to open your WKU email.

Already have a Microsoft account? You can switch between accounts by clicking your Account Manager icon in the top right corner and selecting Sign in with a different account.
4

Forwarding Your WKU TopperMail

We recommend forwarding your WKU email to an address you check regularly so you don't miss important communications from MEC faculty and staff.

  1. In Outlook, click the Settings (gear) icon in the top right corner.

  2. Type "Forwarding" in the search box and select Forwarding from the results.

  3. Toggle on the Enable forwarding option and enter the email address you want mail sent to.

  4. Save your changes.

5

Installing Microsoft Office on Your Device

Your WKU account includes free access to the full Microsoft Office suite for installation on personal devices.

WKU Instructions: Installing Microsoft 365 on a Personal Device →

6

Accessing ROTC Blackboard

All MEC courses are housed in ROTC's Blackboard portal. This is separate from any other Blackboard system you may have used at your home institution. Use your individual login credentials to access the platform.

Open ROTC Blackboard →

If you do not know your ROTC Blackboard username or password, please raise your hand or use the chat to request assistance. If you experience issues logging in after orientation, please contact catie.weaver@wku.edu.
7

Updating Your ROTC Blackboard Email

Course announcements posted by instructors will be sent to the email listed in your ROTC Blackboard account. Please update this to an address you check daily.

  1. In ROTC Blackboard, click on your name, which should appear at the top of the left-hand menu.

  2. Hover over Email Address and click the pencil icon that appears.

  3. Change the email address to the one where you prefer for course communications to be sent.

  4. Click Done.

8

Your MEC Courses

Once you are logged in to ROTC Blackboard, your course list should include the following:

MEC702-2026_MEC Seminar and Orientation
MEC702-2026_WKU Student Portal Summer
MEC702-2026_Teaching and Learning_Super
If one or more of the above courses is missing from your list, please let us know during the orientation session. Additional courses will be added as you progress through the program.
